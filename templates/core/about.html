College Major Project Overview

About SmartKart AI

An Intelligent Grocery Delivery & Household Shopping Management System Using Python & Django.

Problem Statement & Objectives

Traditional quick-commerce platforms focus purely on transactional ordering without assisting consumers with budget overspending, manual grocery list re-typing, recipe ingredient estimation, or food-waste reduction. SmartKart AI bridges this gap by infusing smart, explainable algorithmic intelligence directly into the quick-commerce experience.

Household Budget Management
Tracks delivered & cart spend against user-defined monthly limits with live 80% threshold warnings.
AI List Digitization
Transforms handwritten or printed shopping notes into structured cart items using OCR and token fuzzy matching.
Food Waste Rescue
Near-expiry inventory deals at deep discounts with urgency color-coding to minimize spoilage.
Explainable Product Substitution
6-factor scoring algorithm recommending in-stock alternatives when products are out of stock.

Academic Project Disclaimer

SmartKart AI is designed and implemented exclusively for major project demonstration and academic evaluation. All online payment options (UPI, Credit/Debit Card) and delivery tracking interfaces are fully simulated in software without actual monetary transactions.
